Who We Are

Building a smarter and more sustainable industrial future.

Water Innovation Systems is an ISO 9001:2015 certified multi-product company specializing in the manufacture of water treatment equipment and industrial chemical dosing systems.

As an authorized channel partner of Milton Roy®, we provide dependable metering pump technologies and solutions for a wide range of industrial applications.

With extensive industry experience, technical knowledge and dedicated customer support, we focus on understanding individual process requirements and delivering practical, performance-oriented solutions.

Our products and services aim to improve operational performance, optimize resource utilization, reduce operating costs and support responsible water management.

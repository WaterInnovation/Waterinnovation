Different applications call for different pump technologies. Explore our core dosing pump families and talk with our team about a suitable configuration.

01 / Electromagnetic

EMAG Pumps

Compact, lightweight chemical metering pumps with corrosion-resistant GFPP enclosures on A, B, D and V series. Liquid-end options include PP, PVC, PVDF and SS 316.

  • Composite Fluorofilm diaphragm
  • Pulse / 4–20 mA control on A, B & D
  • Series E explosion-proof option
  • Optional low-level switch
Enquire about EMAG
02 / Mechanical diaphragm

MAD Pumps

Mechanically actuated diaphragm pumps with a simple, flexible design, rugged performance and long-term cost benefits for chemical metering duties.

  • NSF / ANSI 61 certified
  • NSF / ANSI 372 certified
  • Confirm model and duty with our team
Enquire about MAD
03 / Hydraulic diaphragm

HAD Pumps

Hydraulically actuated diaphragm pumps designed for accurate, repeatable flow control, rugged enclosures and application flexibility.

  • M-Roy series
  • MAXROY series
  • PHL series
  • Review application and specifications
Enquire about HAD
04 / Plunger metering

Packed Plunger Pumps

Metering pumps with variable-eccentric stroke adjustment and low-pulsation delivery for high- and low-pressure dosing systems.

  • Conforms to API 675
  • Up to 11,000 LPH at up to 250 bar
  • ±0.5% steady-state accuracy
  • 10:1 turndown ratio
Enquire about packed plunger

More metering pump options

Milton D Pump

A modular industrial metering pump with HPD diaphragm or packed-plunger liquid-end options.

  • Published per-head range: 0.5–53 gph (1.9–200 l/h), at different rated pressures
  • Ratings span 3,000 psig (207 bar) at low flow to 70 psig (5 bar) at high flow
  • ±1.0% accuracy over a 10:1 turndown ratio
  • Multiplex configurations up to six pumps; maximum 25 HP
View Milton D details

LMI Roytronic · Series B

A chemical metering pump with adjustable stroke frequency and options for pulse or 4–20 mA inputs.

  • Up to 1000:1 turndown; NEMA 4X / IP65 enclosure
  • BX1: 6.1 l/h at 10.3 bar · BX2: 9.5 l/h at 6.9 bar
  • BX3: 17.0 l/h at 3.4 bar · BX4: 26.5 l/h at 2.07 bar
  • Output and pressure ratings depend on model and liquid end

LIQUITRON™ DP5000 Series

Dual-Channel pH Controller

Monitor pH with two control channels, programmable response delay and output limit timers. Standard relay or proportional pulse outputs can drive chemical metering pumps.

  • 0.01 pH resolution

Plan your controller setup

Start with the measurement. Build from there.

Controller selection is one part of a complete treatment system. Review the process details and connected equipment before finalizing a configuration.

STEP 01

Define what you need to monitor

Identify whether the control point is conductivity / TDS or pH, and clarify the process operating range.

STEP 02

Review the control response

Consider set points, alarm needs, timer functions and whether relay or proportional pulse output is suitable for the connected dosing equipment.

STEP 03

Check the complete installation

Confirm probes, flow switch, outputs, wiring, mounting environment and required options against the product documentation.

Product features and certifications vary by controller configuration. Please confirm compatibility, supplied accessories and current technical specifications with our team before ordering.

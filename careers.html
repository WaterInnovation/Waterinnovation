⚙ Open position

Service Engineer

We are looking for a skilled and motivated Service Engineer to manage installation, maintenance and troubleshooting of Milton Roy dosing pumps and water treatment systems.

⌖ Bangalore ◷ Full-time ⚙ Technical Service

Key responsibilities

  • Install and commission Milton Roy dosing pumps.
  • Work with Maxroy, mRoy, PNP and PHL pump series.
  • Perform preventive and breakdown maintenance.
  • Diagnose mechanical, hydraulic and electrical issues.
  • Carry out pump calibration and flow adjustment.
  • Provide technical support and customer training.
  • Maintain service reports and technical documentation.
  • Attend customer site visits and field service assignments.

Technical skills and qualifications

  • Diploma / BE / B.Tech in Mechanical, Electrical or Instrumentation Engineering.
  • Knowledge of diaphragm and plunger dosing pumps.
  • Understanding of flow calculation and calibration.
  • Basic electrical troubleshooting knowledge.
  • Familiarity with water treatment and chemical dosing.
  • Ability to understand P&ID drawings and technical manuals.
Experience: 1–4 years; freshers may apply. Apply for this role →

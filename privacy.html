01 Introduction

Welcome to Water Innovation Systems ("Water Innovation", "we", "our" or "us"). We are a Bangalore-based provider of industrial chemical dosing equipment, water treatment solutions, dosing pumps, controllers, valves and process equipment.

This Privacy Policy applies to information collected through our website, including waterinnovation.co.in , our website enquiry forms and communications with our team.

By using our website, you acknowledge that you have had the opportunity to read this policy. Where applicable, we will obtain consent for processing personal information as required by law.

02 Information We Collect

We may collect different types of information depending on how you interact with our website and services.

A. Personal Information

When you submit an enquiry or contact our team, we may collect:

  • Your name and contact details.
  • Your business or company name.
  • Your email address.
  • Your telephone or mobile number.
  • Your project requirements and enquiry details.
  • Information you voluntarily provide in messages or communications.

B. Technical Information

When you browse our website, certain technical information may be collected by the website, hosting provider or enabled third-party services, such as:

  • IP address and general network information.
  • Browser type and version.
  • Device type and operating system.
  • Pages visited and approximate visit duration.
  • Referring website and basic interaction information.
Important: We do not intentionally request sensitive personal information through our general website enquiry form. Please avoid submitting passwords, financial credentials or unnecessary sensitive information.

03 How We Collect Information

Information may be collected through the following methods:

  • Website enquiry forms: When you submit a product, service or sales enquiry.
  • Email communication: When you email our team directly.
  • Telephone communication: When you contact us by phone.
  • Website interactions: Through basic technical information generated while browsing the website.
  • Third-party services: Through hosting, form processing, analytics or other services that may support our website.

We aim to collect only information that is reasonably necessary for the relevant business or website purpose.

04 How We Use Your Information

Information collected may be used for the following purposes:

  • Responding to product and service enquiries.
  • Providing quotations and commercial information.
  • Understanding your technical and application requirements.
  • Communicating about orders, products and services.
  • Providing customer assistance and after-sales support.
  • Improving our website functionality and user experience.
  • Monitoring website performance and identifying technical issues.
  • Maintaining website security and preventing misuse.
  • Meeting applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

We do not intend to sell your personal information to third parties.

05 Cookies & Website Tracking

Cookies are small text files that websites may store on your device to support website functionality and improve the browsing experience.

Depending on the services enabled on our website, cookies or similar technologies may be used for:

  • Essential website functionality.
  • Remembering certain website preferences.
  • Understanding website traffic and performance.
  • Improving website navigation and user experience.

Third-party analytics or embedded services may also use their own cookies or similar technologies where enabled.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Blocking certain cookies may affect some website functions.

Our use of optional cookies and analytics technologies, where applicable, should follow the consent and disclosure requirements relevant to those services and applicable law.

06 Sharing of Information

We may share information with trusted service providers when reasonably necessary to operate our website and conduct our business.

These may include:

  • Website hosting providers: For website storage, hosting and technical operations.
  • Email and communication providers: For receiving and responding to enquiries.
  • Form processing providers: Where an external form service is used to deliver website enquiries.
  • Analytics providers: Where website performance measurement tools are enabled.
  • Professional advisers: Where reasonably necessary for legal, accounting or compliance purposes.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law, a valid legal request or a competent regulatory authority.

We expect relevant service providers to handle information responsibly and only for the purposes for which it is shared.

07 Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, loss or misuse.

Depending on the systems in use, these measures may include:

  • Secure website connections using HTTPS where enabled.
  • Access restrictions for business communication systems.
  • Appropriate password and account protection practices.
  • Website maintenance and security monitoring.
  • Reasonable precautions when handling enquiry information.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We therefore cannot promise absolute security of information.

08 Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including:

  • Responding to enquiries and maintaining business communication.
  • Managing customer and commercial relationships.
  • Maintaining records required for business operations.
  • Meeting applicable legal, accounting and regulatory obligations.
  • Resolving disputes and protecting legitimate business interests.

When information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to delete, anonymise or securely dispose of it, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

09 Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information.

These may include the right to:

  • Request information about personal data we process.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of personal information where legally applicable.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Raise concerns or submit a privacy-related complaint.
  • Exercise other rights available under applicable data protection laws.

To make a privacy-related request, please contact us using the email address provided in the Contact Us section. We may need to verify your identity before processing certain requests.

Requests will be handled in accordance with applicable legal requirements and any relevant exceptions.

10 Third-Party Websites and Services

Our website may contain links to external websites, product resources, social media platforms or other third-party services.

These external websites operate independently and may have their own privacy policies, cookie practices and terms of use.

Water Innovation Systems does not control the privacy practices or content of external websites. We encourage visitors to review the privacy policies of third-party websites before sharing personal information with them.

11 Children's Privacy

Our website and services are primarily intended for businesses, professionals and individuals seeking industrial equipment and related solutions.

We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children in connection with our business enquiry services.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through our website, please contact us so that we can review the matter and take appropriate action as required by applicable law.

12 Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our website, business practices, technology or applicable legal requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page. The effective date displayed at the top of the policy will indicate when the latest version takes effect.

We encourage visitors to review this page periodically to stay informed about our privacy practices.

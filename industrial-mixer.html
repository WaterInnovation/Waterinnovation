Designed to mount at a tank’s side flange, these mixers use a small-diameter SABRE® impeller to blend large tank volumes with reduced energy consumption. CFD analysis can help predict tank flow and identify potential dead zones.

Selection guidance

Match the mixer to the tank and duty.

Each range serves different tank layouts and mixing needs. To help identify a suitable option, share:

  • 01 Tank capacity, dimensions and available mounting arrangement
  • 02 Liquid viscosity, specific gravity, temperature and solids content
  • 03 Mixing objective: blending, circulation, flocculation or keeping solids in suspension
  • 04 Operating conditions, including filling or emptying cycles and any speed-control needs

Published model ranges and performance depend on the mixer configuration and application. Confirm suitability with our team.
